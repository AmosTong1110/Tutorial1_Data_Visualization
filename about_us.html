Useful context for better decisions.

An educational project about appliance energy use in Australia.

Energy information should be clear enough to use at home and when comparing bills. We use simple explanations and indicative comparisons to make energy information easier to use. Always check official product labels. Climate, homes, electricity plans, and viewing habits affect results.

Project principles

Make it legible

Use language people can act on.

Show the assumptions

Keep estimates illustrative and use official data.

Keep it local

Focus on appliances in Australian homes.
