About / Power AU

Useful context for better decisions.

Power AU is a placeholder educational project about appliance energy consumption in the Australian market.

Energy information works best when it is clear enough to use at the shelf, at home, and on the next electricity bill.

What we are exploring

We collect simple explanations and indicative comparisons to make appliance energy conversations less intimidating. The aim is not to replace official product labels or energy advice, but to help people know what to look for.

Our placeholder content focuses on the Australian context: different climates, homes, electricity plans, and viewing habits can all change the real-world result.

Project principles

Make it legible

Translate technical measures into language people can act on.

Show the assumptions

Keep estimates visibly illustrative and point people back to official data.

Keep it local

Talk about appliances as they are bought and used in Australian homes.
